# Motorway - UK’s Fastest Growing Used Car Marketplace

Motorway is the UK’s fastest-growing used car marketplace. We connect people selling their car directly with our network of more than 8,000 verified car dealers, matching each seller on our platform with the dealer who most wants to buy their car.

We help car owners secure a great price online, with their car collected from home, while supporting our car dealer partners to easily acquire the best used car stock.

## About Motorway

Founded in July 2017 by Tom Leathes, Alex Buttle, and Harry Jones, Motorway has revolutionised the UK used car market. Over 1 million people have used Motorway to help sell their car, and we’ve supported thousands of dealers in growing their businesses. Based in London and Brighton, we’re a unicorn startup valued at over £1 billion.

Selling a car hasn't traditionally been easy to navigate. Motorway was created to change that – and it's all we’ve ever done.

Selling your car doesn’t need to be complicated. That’s why we guide you throughout, with expertise, tools, and support.

## How It Works

**For Car Sellers:**
- Get instant valuation using smart tech and live market data
- Expert, AI-driven technology builds your sale-ready profile via the mobile app.
- Set guide price with Motorway team
- 8,000 verified dealers compete in daily online auction
- Accept highest offer
- Nationwide collection at a time that suits you
- Same-day payment into bank account
- No sale, no fees

**For Dealers:**
- Access to high-quality, privately-owned vehicles
- Daily auctions with detailed car profiles
- Automatic bidding system with £1 increments
- End-to-end collection and payment services
- Verified seller information and condition reports
